# Portfolio evidence policy

This portfolio distinguishes between public, verifiable work and private work that cannot be disclosed.

- Claims about a public project link to its repository or another public artefact.
- AI tools may assist research, drafting, implementation or media generation. Final review, validation and delivery remain human-led.
- Client names, data, credentials, internal URLs, private infrastructure, logs and confidential code are never published.
- Metrics are only published when their scope, period and method can be explained. A demo is labelled as a demo and is not represented as a client production deployment.
- Visuals, audio, fonts, stock assets and generated media must be publishable under their relevant rights or licences.
